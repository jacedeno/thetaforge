## Challenge

**Main challenge: Options Alpha Agents.** Build an autonomous AI trading agent designed to generate P&L using Alpaca's trading platform. Develop a clear, testable trading strategy and demonstrate how the agent identifies opportunities, makes trading decisions, manages positions, and performs over the course of the competition. Approaches may include options, trading agents, portfolio income, or anything else Alpaca supports.

### Core requirements (mandatory)

1. **Autonomous agents** — must build autonomous AI trading agents using Alpaca's **Trading API**.
2. **MCP or CLI** — the project must use either Alpaca's **MCP server** or its **CLI tools**.
3. **Options trading** — **all strategies must incorporate options trading.**
4. Everything runs in the **paper trading environment** (simulated funds, real market data — no real money).

### Account requirements

- **Development:** any paper account may be used to explore, prototype and test.
- **Judging (mandatory):** the final submission must run on a **brand-new Alpaca paper trading account created specifically for this hackathon**. Projects run on an existing or reused account **will not be eligible for judging**.
- **Competition account starting balance must be set to $100,000.**
- The **Alpaca paper account ID** must be included in the submission so judges can identify trading activity and evaluate P&L.

### Additional requirements

- **One-page write-up** covering: the AI logic, the risk gates, and the Alpaca infrastructure implementation.
- Submissions must be **original and MIT-compliant** (per prize terms).

## Judging criteria

1. **P&L Performance** — trading performance of the agent in the Alpaca paper environment; P&L and how effectively the strategy performs through its trading activity.
2. **Technology Implementation** — how effectively the project uses Alpaca's Trading API, MCP server, CLI and other required technologies to build an autonomous agent.
3. **Creativity & Originality** — originality of concept, trading strategy, agent behavior, and overall approach.
4. **Presentation & Execution** — clarity of communication, agent demo, and the reasoning behind the strategy and results.
5. **Social Engagement** — quality of the content plus the engagement it generates (for the extra challenge).

Judging is final (skill contest).

## Key takeaways for our entry

- **Options are mandatory** — a pure equity strategy (like ML30 as-is) does not qualify; the strategy must incorporate options trading.
- **MCP server or CLI usage is mandatory** — the agent must trade through Alpaca's MCP server and/or CLI, not just the raw REST API.
- **Fresh paper account for submission** — reusing the existing V1-5m account is not eligible; it must be reset/recreated as a brand-new paper account with exactly **$100,000** starting balance, and its account ID goes in the submission.
- **P&L is a first-class judging criterion** — the agent should trade live (paper) during Aug 28 – Sep 4 to build a track record.
- **Deliverables checklist:** public GitHub repo · demo URL · video · slides · cover image · one-page write-up (AI logic, risk gates, Alpaca infra) · account ID · up to 5 social posts (optional).
